About Us

Wanderer's Nest is a laid-back hostel built for travelers who want to meet new people, sleep comfortably, and explore without breaking the bank. Whether you're backpacking solo or with friends, our common areas, free breakfast, and rooftop hangout spot make it easy to feel at home.

  • ✔ Free Wi-Fi throughout the hostel
  • ✔ 24-hour reception and self check-in
  • ✔ Free walking tours every morning
  • ✔ Female-only dorms available

Our Story

The friendly faces behind the hostel — and the little on-site cafe they run.

Wanderer's Nest started as a small dream between two people who love good coffee, good company, and meeting travelers from every corner of the world. Today, Kushal and Geetika run both the hostel and the cozy cafe downstairs — the heart of the whole place.

Kushal

The friendly face you'll usually find behind the counter or swapping travel stories with guests over a cup of filter coffee. Kushal handles the day-to-day running of the hostel and cafe, and is always happy to point you toward the best hidden spots in the city.

Geetika

The creative force behind the cafe's menu and the warm, homely feel of the common spaces. Geetika loves cooking up comfort food for guests and making sure everyone — local or traveler — feels instantly welcome.
