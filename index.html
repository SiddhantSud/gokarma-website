About Aangan

A Hilltop Basecamp for Backpackers

Aangan by Gokarma Living is a laid-back hostel and cafe set on Kudle hilltop, minutes from Kudle Beach and Gokarna's old town. It's built for travelers who want to slow down, meet people, and eat well without spending much — with resident cats, an occasional goat at the gate, and home-style food guests keep talking about.

  • ✔ Free Wi-Fi in common areas
  • ✔ Garden with badminton & board games
  • ✔ Home-style cafe: breakfast, snacks & South Indian classics
  • ✔ Free parking & luggage storage
