# Content Questionnaire — Our Story & Testimonials

Use this to gather real quotes and details for the website. You don't need to
ask every question — pick whichever fit the conversation naturally.

---

## For the Owners: Kushal & Geetika

**Origin story**
1. How did the two of you decide to open a hostel with a cafe attached?
2. What were you both doing before this? What made you switch to hostel/cafe life?
3. Is there a story behind the name "Wanderer's Nest"? (or whatever you land on)
4. What was the hardest part of getting started?

**The day-to-day**
5. What does a normal day look like for each of you?
6. Kushal — what's your role day-to-day? What part of the job do you enjoy most?
7. Geetika — same question for you. What's your role, and what part do you enjoy most?
8. Is there a signature dish or drink on the cafe menu with a story behind it?

**The people side**
9. What do you want a guest to feel the moment they walk in?
10. Any favorite memory with a guest or traveler that's stuck with you?
11. What's something guests are surprised to learn about the place?
12. If you had to describe the hostel/cafe in three words, what would they be?

**Practical / for the website**
13. Anything you specifically want visitors to know before they book?
14. Any photos you'd like included (of the two of you, the cafe, the space)?

---

## For Happy Customers / Guests

Keep this short and casual — 3-4 questions is usually enough for a good quote.

1. What brought you to [hostel name] / this cafe?
2. What was your favorite part of staying here (or visiting the cafe)?
3. Was there a moment that surprised you, in a good way?
4. How would you describe the vibe/atmosphere in a few words?
5. Would you recommend it to other travelers? Why?
6. Anything specific — a dish, a staff member, a conversation — that stood out?

**Before you publish their answer, always confirm:**
- "Is it okay if we quote you on our website?"
- "How would you like to be credited?" (full name, first name + country, or anonymous)
- "Mind if we use a photo of you too?" (optional, only if you have one)

---

## Tips for using the answers

- Pull the best 1–2 sentences as a direct quote rather than the whole answer.
- Keep the owners' story section warm and personal — it's fine if it reads like
  they're talking directly to the guest.
- For testimonials, 3-6 short quotes from different guests work better on the
  page than 1-2 long ones.
