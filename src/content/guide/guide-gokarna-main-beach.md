Main Beach is Gokarna's town beach, about a 15-minute walk from Aangan. It's where the town meets the sea: pilgrims after a temple visit, families, fishing boats and travellers setting off on the coastal walk.

## Car Street to the sea

The main road through the old town is Car Street, named after the huge temple chariot that's kept there and pulled through the streets at festival time. Walk it to the end and you're on the sand. It's lined with small eateries serving South Indian staples, and makes a good breakfast stop.

## A busy, local beach

Main Beach is more lively than scenic, and its waves are often stronger than Kudle's, so be careful about swimming. It's a lovely place for an evening walk, and the sunsets here are dramatic too.

## The start of the beach trek

The famous Gokarna beach trek starts here. Head south over the headland to Kudle, then on to Om, Half Moon and Paradise Beach. The whole route takes most of a day, so start early, carry plenty of water, and walk it in the cooler months, from October to February.
