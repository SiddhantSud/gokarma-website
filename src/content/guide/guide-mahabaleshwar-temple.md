Gokarna grew up around the Mahabaleshwar Temple, and the old temple town is about a 15-minute walk from Aangan. Even if you're here for the beaches, it's worth one early morning.

## The story of the Atmalinga

The legend goes that Ravana was carrying the Atmalinga, a powerful linga of Shiva, home to Lanka. The gods tricked him into setting it down here: Ganesha, disguised as a young boy, held it for him and placed it on the ground, where it stayed for good. "Mahabaleshwar" means the all-powerful lord.

## Visiting

- **Hours:** roughly 6 AM to 12:30 PM and 5 PM to 8 PM, every day. Entry is free.
- **Dress:** traditional dress is expected. Men usually wear a dhoti or lungi and may be asked to remove their shirt; women wear a saree or salwar kameez. Shorts and jeans aren't allowed.
- **Inside:** photography isn't allowed, and non-Hindus may be kept out of the inner sanctum.
- **Timing:** mornings are calmest; festival days get very crowded.

Rules are set by the temple and can change, so check at the gate before you go in.

## Around the temple

The streets around the temple are the oldest part of Gokarna, with small shops, sacred tanks and the town beach just beyond. Ram Teertha, a spring-fed pond near the temple, is a quiet spot that many visitors never find.
