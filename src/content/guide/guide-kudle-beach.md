Kudle is the beach right below Aangan: a long curve of sand between two green headlands, about 10 to 15 minutes' walk down the hill. It's the relaxed one of Gokarna's beaches, good for a morning swim, a long walk, or doing very little at all.

## Swimming and safety

On most days the water at Kudle is calmer than at Gokarna's main town beach, but the sea here changes with the season. Swim where others are swimming, stay out when locals warn you off, and skip it entirely during the monsoon.

## Food and evenings

The back of the beach is lined with cafes and guesthouses, which makes Kudle the easy choice for a sunset drink and dinner. It faces west, so the sun goes down right over the water.

## Part of the coastal walk

Kudle sits on the famous Gokarna beach trek, the path that links Main Beach, Kudle, Om, Half Moon and Paradise. Om Beach is about a 20-minute walk from Kudle's southern end. Start early, carry water, and save the walk for the cooler months between October and February.
