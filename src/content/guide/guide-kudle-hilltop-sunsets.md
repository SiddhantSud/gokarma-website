Kudle is the beach in Gokarna that faces west, which is why its hilltop is where people gather in the evening. From Aangan it's about five minutes on foot, so you can leave after a game of cards and still be early.

## When to go

Aim to be up there 30 to 45 minutes before sunset. The light over the sea changes fast in the last half hour, and the good rocks fill up. October to February usually gives the clearest skies; in the monsoon the sea is wild and the paths are slippery.

## Finding your spot

The hillock above the steps down to Kudle Beach looks straight over the bay, and quieter rocks sit along the cliff towards Om Beach. Stay on the open paths: parts of the hill are private property, so don't climb fences or cut through gated land.

## What to bring

- Water and a light layer: it gets breezy on the cliff once the sun drops
- A phone torch for the walk back after dark
- Your rubbish, carried home with you

Travellers who have written about it describe the hilltop sunset as one of the quieter, better ones in Gokarna, a calm alternative to the busy beach-side cafes.
