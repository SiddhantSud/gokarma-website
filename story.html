Our Story

Meet Kushal & Geetika

Aangan by Gokarma Living started as a small dream between two people who love good food, good company, and meeting travelers from every corner of the world. Today, Kushal and Geetika run both the hostel and the cozy cafe on-site — home to resident cats, the odd curious goat at the gate, and a menu guests keep coming back for (the Karam Podi Dosa has quite the following).

Kushal

The friendly face you'll usually find behind the counter or swapping travel stories with guests. Kushal handles the day-to-day running of the hostel and cafe, and guests often mention how quickly he makes a new arrival feel at home — several reviews single him out by name for going out of his way to help plan the day's beach trek or track down a missed bus.

Geetika

The warmth behind the common spaces and the care behind the cafe's menu. Guests fondly call her "Geet" — and more than a few have said they wish they'd booked a longer stay once they got to know her.
